CV4 craniosacral therapy device

A machine that delivers a hands-on therapy technique consistently, in a clinic or at home.

Status
Ongoing; working units in use
Partner
Jorge Fernandez, manual therapist
My role
Design and engineering
Tools
Fusion 360, 3D printing, ESP32, iOS

Why this project

After a back injury a few years ago, I was fortunate to meet Jorge Fernandez, an exceptional manual therapist who helped me recover. He had an idea for a craniosacral therapy device that could make these treatments far more accessible but, because of his blindness, couldn’t develop it himself. I have been working with him on it since.

What it does

The device applies targeted pressure to the cranial base, letting therapists deliver CV4 therapy with a more consistent outcome. It is also designed for patients to use at home.

My role

  • Mechanical design

    Every part is modelled in Fusion 360 and proven through rounds of 3D-printed prototypes, reviewed with Jorge after each version.

  • Actuation

    Designed the spring and magnet actuation that controls how the device applies pressure.

  • Firmware and app

    Motorized positioning runs on custom ESP32 firmware, controlled from a companion iOS app.

  • Production

    Built fully equipped working units, now in use by Jorge, and am planning the bill of materials and production for a run of about 10,000 units.
